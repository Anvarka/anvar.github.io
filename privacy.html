1. General Information

The Romanov Dynasty application is an educational reference guide about the history of the Romanov dynasty. We respect your privacy and do not collect personal data.

2. Data We Collect

Personal Data: We do not collect any personal data.

The application does not request your name, email address, phone number, location, or any other personal information.

Data Stored on Your Device: The application stores the following data locally on your device:

  • a list of rulers marked as favorites;
  • a list of rulers you have viewed.

This data does not leave your device and is not shared with third parties. It is deleted when you uninstall the application.

3. Network Activity

The application loads illustrations (portraits and paintings) from public websites, including Wikimedia Commons and other sources, for display in the gallery. When these requests are made, your IP address may be visible to the image provider in accordance with its privacy policy. We do not collect or store information about these requests.

When you tap the "Learn More" or "Open in Map" links, the application opens an external web browser or mapping application. Once you leave the application, the privacy policies of those third-party services apply.

4. Advertising and Analytics

The application does not contain:

  • advertising;
  • third-party trackers;
  • analytics SDKs;
  • in-app purchases.

5. Children

The application is suitable for users of all ages. We do not collect personal information from children.
